# Your Year in Tech

> Your Year in Tech — a free, self-paced, community-supported full-stack curriculum built for people with real lives.

---

## What this is

Your Year in Tech is a yearlong guidebook for "developing the developer" — a self-taught rubric for teaching yourself to code and developing a software engineer's mindset. It covers the PERN stack (PostgreSQL, Express, React, Node.js) plus MongoDB exposure, professional development, and life skills that help to expand your capacity: compatible day jobs, self care, mutual aid, workplace realities, and what to do if coding turns out not to be for you, among other ideas.

It's a work in progress, and we welcome your feedback. Engage!

It is free. It will always be free.

---

## Who it is for

This curriculum was built with people in mind who are:

- Working a job while trying to change careers
- Raising kids, managing health, or carrying other real responsibilities
- Coming from non-traditional backgrounds with no CS degree

If you have 15–20 hours a week and the capacity to persist and keep moving forward, this was built for you.

- Active recall with Anki
- Hands-on projects every week — done means built, not watched

- Mini capstone and full capstone projects

### Professional development
- Technical interview prep
- Resume, GitHub profile, LinkedIn, portfolio
- Public speaking and demos

### Life skills
- Compatible day jobs while studying
- Internships — how to find them without a degree

### The human touch

- Python intro
- AI and ML fundamentals
- Career placement options
- Alumni next steps

## How it works

**At your own pace.** The yearlong plan is a compass. Whether you finish in 40 weeks or a year — what matters is real understanding.


**Done means built, not watched.** Comprehension is proven through shipped work. Projects and deliverables confirm understanding.

**Active recall daily.** 10 minutes of Anki review every morning. One card per concept, written in your own words, made the day you learn it. Spaced repetition does the memory work so your study sessions can focus on building.

## Inspiration and prior art

This curriculum exists in a genre with deep roots. Programs that came before and helped shape the landscape this builds on:

- **freeCodeCamp** (2014) — free, community-supported, full-stack JavaScript
- **The Odin Project** (2013) — free, rigorous, self-paced
- **Resilient Coders** — community-focused, tuition-free, MIT licensed
- **Techtonica** — free software engineering training for women and non-binary adults
- **CodePath** — free mobile and web development courses
- **Per Scholas** — free tech training for underrepresented communities
- **100Devs / Leon Noel** — free community program, pacing philosophy and community-first approach

Your Year in Tech is not a fork of any single program. It draws from the whole ecosystem and adds layers — life skills, mental health, whole-person support, and pivot paths — that are not present in most technical curricula.

## What we're exploring

Most of what you will find elsewhere covers the technical stack. What is different here:

- The whole-life framing — self care, relationships, compatible day jobs, mutual aid
- Real talk sections — diversity, workplace bullying, HR as structural reality
- Math and critical thinking as explicit foundational prerequisites
- Whimsy and mental health as load-bearing curriculum elements, not afterthoughts
- Pivot paths for students who change direction
- Networking for shy people
- Loving problems as a frame for entrepreneurship and hiring
- Monetization woven in early, not saved for graduation
- Active recall with Anki explicitly structured into weekly practice
- Mini capstone before full capstone sequencing
- The The Common Room community channel
